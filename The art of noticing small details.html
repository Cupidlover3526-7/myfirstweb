THE ART OF NOTICING SMALL THINGS

Have you ever noticed something small that others just walk past it which somehow made you day a WHOLE LOT BETTER?

Yeah, I know it happens just Sometimes like once in a blue moon. Sometimes we're having such a bad day that we just wish something like that would happen again, right?

I know that people are very busy and often forget that small details in their day.

Sometimes we are so busy thinking about school, friends, homeworks, family or just what we have to do next that we completely forget to look around us.
We don't notice the small, little things like how the sun looks during sunset, how the leaves make different shapes on their shadows, how different people smile when they are genuinely happy, how the rain sounds or even how peaceful it is for a few seconds sometimes when theres completely silence.

I think noticing small details makes life more interesting. Even normal days can have little moments that we usually ignore. A funny thing your friend says, your favorite song playing at the perfect time, or the smell of rain after a hot day can become something you remember. The small things might not seem important at first, but they are everywhere. We just have to slow down and actually notice them.
